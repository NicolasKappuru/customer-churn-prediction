The central question for this workshop is: What does your data look like, and
what performance can you achieve with well-engineered baseline models?
Your answers will establish the difficulty of the problem, the degree of class imbalance,
data quality characteristics, and baseline performance metrics that serve as reference
points for comparative analysis.



General Workshop Definition: Data preparation and supervised learning baselines involve
downloading, validating, preprocessing, and characterising the assigned dataset, then
establishing rigorous baseline models using traditional machine learning techniques applied
to appropriate feature representations. The goal is complete mastery of the data, its structure,
and its challenges before proceeding to more complex approaches.


Dataset Download and Integrity Verification: Download the assigned
dataset, verify data integrity, and document all access steps reproducibly.


Dataset Access and Acquisition
Each group has been assigned a specific dataset for your domain. All datasets
are freely available — no paid subscriptions are required. Your instructor has provided
dataset-specific information separately. General principles for dataset acquisition:

1. Verify Dataset Legitimacy and License:
• Confirm you have legal access to the dataset with appropriate licenses and permissions
• Read and understand any usage terms, restrictions, or citations required by the
dataset provider
• Verify dataset size and storage requirements on your system
2. Download and Setup:
• Document all download procedures in your README.md for reproducibility
• Verify file integrity if checksums are provided
• Organize files in a consistent directory structure (e.g., data/raw/,
data/processed/)
3. Understand Your Data Format:
• Identify what types of files your dataset contains (images, CSV, JSON, timeseries,
text, etc.)
• Understand the label/target format and class definitions
• Note any metadata, annotations, or auxiliary files
